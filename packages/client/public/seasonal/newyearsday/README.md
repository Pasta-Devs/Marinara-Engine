# New Year's Day seasonal art

Decorations for the New Year's Day seasonal theme: the morning-after cluster on Home and the art in the seasonal greeting.

Provenance: generated for Marinara Engine with AI image generation on 2026-10-30, for this theme: knocked-over red cups, a crushed party hat, empty chips bowl, deflated balloon and fizzy water; a glass of water with a fizzing tablet; and one sad half-deflated balloon. Each picture has a transparent background, was trimmed to its artwork, resized and saved as WebP (quality 82):
