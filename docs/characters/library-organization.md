# Organizing Your Character Library

The **Characters** page in the Library (on the right) lists every character you have created or imported. Use it to search, sort, group characters into folders, mark favorites, filter by tags, and export or delete many at once.

## The Characters page

Open **Library → Characters**. The page shows your characters as portrait cards. Along the top:

- **Browse online** opens the [Card Browser](bot-browser.md) to find characters on public sites.
- **Import** brings in a character file. See [Importing and Exporting Character Cards](import-export.md).
- **New character** opens the **Create Character** window, blank or from a template. The **New character** card in the grid does the same.
- **Search characters or tag:name** filters the cards (see [Search](#search)).
- **Favourites**, **Recently edited** and a chip for each tag narrow the cards. On a phone these are one **Filter** list.
- The sort menu offers **A to Z**, **Z to A**, **Newest**, **Oldest** and **My order**.
- The three size buttons make the cards small, medium or large.
- **New folder** (the folder icon) and **Select** (the check icon) sit next to the page title.

Click a card to open the character. Its **Chat** button starts a chat with that character. With many characters, click **Load more** at the bottom.

### A card's actions

Hover a card and click **⋯** in its top-left corner, or right-click the card. On a touch screen, press and hold the card. The menu has:

- **Duplicate** makes a copy of the character.
- **Move to folder** puts the character in a folder, or takes it out with **No folder**.
- **Export** downloads the character in Marinara's native format.
- **Select** starts selecting, with this card already picked.
- **Delete** removes the character after you confirm.

### Your own order

Drag a card and drop it before or after another card. The sort menu switches to **My order** and keeps that order until you pick another sort. Choose **My order** again to go back to it. The classic panel's **Manual order** is the same order.

### Folders

1. Click **New folder** next to the page title. A folder named **unnamed** appears above the cards.
2. To rename it, click **⋯** on the folder and choose **Rename**.
3. Drag a card onto the folder, or use **Move to folder** in the card's menu.

Click a folder's name to fold or unfold it. The number next to the name is how many characters it holds. **Delete folder** in the folder's **⋯** menu removes the folder only: its characters move back out to the page.

### Export or delete many characters

1. Click **Select** next to the page title. A check box appears on each card.
2. Click the cards you want. A bar at the bottom shows how many are selected, with **Select all**.
3. Choose **Move to folder**, **Export** or **Delete** in the bar.

**Export** downloads one zip named `marinara-characters.zip`. **Delete** asks you to confirm first. Click **X** in the bar or press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to stop selecting.

The **Favorites first** sort and deleting a tag from every character are in the classic layout's Characters panel, described below. Switch to it with **New layout** in **Settings → Appearance → Theme**, and back the same way.

## In the classic layout: the Characters panel

From the top of the panel you can:

- Click **Download** to open the [Card Browser](bot-browser.md) and find characters on public sites.
- Click **Open Library** to open a larger full-page grid of the same characters.
- Click **New** (the plus icon) to open the **Create Character** window.
- Click **Import** (the download icon) to import a character file. See [Importing and Exporting Character Cards](import-export.md).
- Click **Select** (the check icon) to turn on selection mode for bulk actions.

The full library keeps your selected card, sort order and scroll position when you open a character for editing and come back.

Each row shows the avatar, name, an optional title line, the creator and version, up to 3 tags, and a rough token estimate. A small star badge marks a favorite. Hover a row to see **Duplicate** and **Delete**. If you have many characters, click **Load more** at the bottom to load the next page.

## Search

Type in the search box at the top. The placeholder reads **Search characters or tag:name** (**Search characters/tags** in the classic layout). Plain text matches a character's name, title, description and tags, ignoring case. Typing `knight` shows every character with "knight" in any of those.

| Search | What it finds |
| --- | --- |
| `male genshin` or `male,genshin` | Cards that have both exact tags. |
| `male "Modern AU"` | Quote a tag that has spaces or commas. |
| `-tag:"slow burn"` | Hides every card with that tag, even if the rest of your search matches. |
| `-vampire` | The same, for a one-word tag. You can drop the quotes. |
| `mage -tag:"villain"` | Cards matching "mage", without the ones tagged "villain". |

Ordinary phrase searches still work. The same search works in the full-page Character Library and for personas.

## Sort (classic layout)

The classic panel's sort dropdown has:

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Manual order** | The order you set yourself. |
| **A-Z** | Names from A to Z. |
| **Z-A** | Names from Z to A. |
| **Newest** | Most recently created first. |
| **Oldest** | Oldest created first. |
| **Favorites** | Favorites first, then the rest. |

To set a manual order, drag a row up or down, or press <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Up</kbd> or <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Down</kbd> on a focused row. Either switches the sort to **Manual order**.

## Folders (classic layout)

Folders group related characters, for example by story or series, so a long list is easier to scan. They are optional, and the new layout shows the same folders.

1. Click **New Folder**. A folder named **unnamed** appears.
2. Rename it: double-click or double-tap it, or select it and press <kbd>F2</kbd>. Type the new name and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.
3. Drag a character row onto the folder to put it inside. Once you have a folder, a helper line reads **Drag and drop characters to folders, double-click or double-tap to rename**.

To take a character out, hover its row inside the folder and click the remove-from-folder button, or drag it out. Click a folder to expand or collapse it. The number next to its name is how many characters it holds.

On a touch screen, press and hold a row to pick it up, then drag. A normal swipe still scrolls.

To move several at once, hold one row and tap other rows with a second finger to carry them along as a stack. Then drop the stack on a folder.

To delete a folder, hover it and click its trash button. A folder with characters asks you to confirm: **Delete "name"? Its N characters will stay in the library and move out of the folder.** An empty folder is removed at once. Deleting a folder never deletes the characters in it.

## Use a folder as a group chat roster

Each folder is also a saved roster for group chats. When you set up a chat with more than one character, **Add from Folder** adds every character in a folder in one step. See [Group Chats and Group Conversations](../chats/group-chats.md).

## Favorites and tags

### Favorites

Open a character and click its **Favorite** star to turn it on or off. On the Characters page, the **Favourites** chip shows only favorites. In the classic panel, favorites show a small star badge, and three buttons under the search box filter the list:

- **All** shows every character.
- **Favs** shows only favorites.
- **Non-favs** shows only characters that are not favorites.

You can also pick **Favorites** in the sort dropdown to put favorites at the top.

### Tags

Tags are labels such as `fantasy` or `slow burn`. You add them in the character editor. See [Creating and Editing Characters](creating-and-editing-characters.md#metadata-tab). On the Characters page, click a tag's chip to show only characters with that tag. In the classic panel, click a tag chip on any row to filter the list the same way.

In the classic panel, when your characters have tags, a **Tags** button with a count appears, such as **Tags (12)**. Click it to expand the full list:

- Click a tag to include it as a filter. With several tags selected, a character matches if it has any of them.
- Click the X on a tag to delete that tag from every character that has it. You are asked to confirm: **Remove tag "name" from all characters?**
- **Clear** appears once a tag filter is active. Click it to clear the tag filters.

To exclude a tag instead, use `-tag:` in the search box, as shown in [Search](#search).

## Export or delete many characters (classic layout)

1. Click **Select** at the top of the panel. A checkbox appears on each row.
2. Click the characters you want. The panel header shows how many are selected.
3. Use the action bar at the bottom.

The action bar has two buttons:

- **Export** downloads the selected characters as one zip named `marinara-characters.zip`, in Marinara's native format.
- **Delete** removes the selected characters. You are asked first: **Delete N characters?**

In selection mode you can also drag the selected characters into a folder together. For file formats, see [Importing and Exporting Character Cards](import-export.md).
