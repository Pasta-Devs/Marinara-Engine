# Getting Started with Marinara Engine

Marinara Engine is an app for chatting and roleplaying with AI characters. It runs on your own computer. This guide shows the Home screen and the steps to your first chat.

## Start your first chat

1. Install Marinara Engine and open it in your browser. Pick your platform in [Marinara Engine Installation](../INSTALLATION.md).
2. Add an account so the app can reach an AI model. An account is one API key (a secret code, like a password, from an AI service) and the models you pick. See [Connecting to an AI Provider](../connections/connecting-to-a-provider.md).
3. Make or import a character. Open the **Characters** panel, then click **New** to open the **Create Character** window, or click **Import** to bring in a card from a file. See [Creating and Editing Characters](../characters/creating-and-editing-characters.md).
4. Start a chat. In the chat sidebar, pick a mode tab, then click the **+** button. It is named **New Conversation**, **New Roleplay** or **New Game**, to match the tab.
5. Follow the setup window that opens, then click **Create Chat**.

If it did not work:

- No account yet: the add-account window opens first and ends with **Start chatting**.
- A **Set Up** window asks you to choose a model: pick one from the list, then click **Create Chat**.

Marinara does not include an online AI. Most people connect to an online service. You can also download the **Built-in model**, a small model that runs on your computer with no API key. It suits light helper jobs, not your main chat. See [Built-in model setup](../connections/local-model.md).

The first time you open Marinara, a short guided tour starts. See [The First-Time Tutorial](tutorial.md). Stuck on a step? Ask Professor Mari, the built-in assistant: [Professor Mari, Your In-App Assistant](professor-mari.md).

## The Home screen at a glance

The Home screen shows when no chat is open. It looks like a web browser: tabs and bookmarks at the top, widgets below.

- **Tabs.** **Home** is the main page. **Professor** opens Professor Mari. Some installed agents add a tab of their own.
- **Search bar.** On a computer, click the bar under the tabs, or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> on a Mac), to find chats, characters, settings and more. Phones, tablets and narrow windows do not show this bar.
- **Bookmarks.** Quick links to **Discord**, **Support**, **Credits**, **Documentation**, **Tutorial**, **FAQ**, **Achievements** and **Widgets**. **Achievements** shows only when the Achievements setting is on. Bookmarks show only on the **Home** tab. On a phone, tablet or narrow window, tap the bookmark button at the left of the tabs to see them as a list.

In the middle you see the logo, **Marinara Engine** and the version number. Below them, the **Conversation**, **Roleplay** and **Game** buttons each start a new chat in that mode.

## Choose a chat mode

You pick a mode when you start a chat.

- **Conversation**: a plain AI chat, styled like a messaging app. See [Conversation Mode: Getting Started](../conversation/getting-started.md).
- **Roleplay**: roleplay with characters, scene tracking and world state. See [Roleplay Mode: Getting Started](../roleplay/getting-started.md).
- **Game**: a single-player game run by an AI Game Master, with a party, dice, maps and quests. See [Game Mode: Getting Started](../game/getting-started.md).

## Show, hide and move Home widgets

Widgets are small cards you can show, hide and move.

1. Click **Widgets** in the bookmarks. The **Home Widgets** window opens. It lists widgets under **Built-in**, **Agents** and **Personal**.
2. Click the eye button beside a widget to show or hide it. A widget you show again goes to the first free place on Home.
3. To move a widget, drag the handle in its top-right corner. On a computer, the handle appears when you point at the widget. With a keyboard, focus the handle and use the arrow keys.

Your layout is saved in this browser. Personal widgets also have buttons to edit or delete them. At the bottom of the window, **Browser chrome** has one switch: **Show URL bar** shows or hides the search bar on a computer.

Professor Mari can make a personal widget for you if you ask her. Installed agents can add widgets too.

## Reference: built-in widgets

| Widget | What it does | Shown at first |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Professor Mari** | **Ask Professor Mari** opens her chat. | Yes |
| **Recent chats** | Shows your latest chats with their last message. Click one to reopen it. With no chats, it says **Your chats will show here**. | Yes |
| **Learn the engine** | Links to **Documentation**, **Replay tutorial** and **Read FAQ**. | Yes |
| **What's New** | **See what's new** opens the What's New window for this version. **Read release notes** opens the full notes on the web. | Yes |
| **Community** | Links to **Discord**, **Support** and **Credits**. | Yes |
| **Character Library** | A fresh selection of your characters each day. | No |
| **Discovery desk** | Suggests agents you have not installed. Use the arrows for more, or **Browse agents** to open the catalog. | No |
| **Character of the Day** | One character from your library, with **Start a chat** and **View character**. | No |
| **Clock & calendar** | Today's date and time. | No |
| **Achievements** | Your latest and closest achievements. Needs the Achievements setting on. | No |

## Where to get help

- Ask Professor Mari on the Home screen.
- Open the **FAQ** bookmark for quick answers.
- Open the **Documentation** bookmark for the in-app guides.
- Click **Discord** to reach the community, or **Support** to back the project.

If something is broken, see [Troubleshooting Marinara Engine](../TROUBLESHOOTING.md).
