# Getting Started with Marinara Engine

Welcome to Marinara Engine. This guide covers what the app is, what you see on the Home screen, and the first steps to start your first chat. It is written for a new user, so no prior setup knowledge is needed.

## What Marinara Engine is

Marinara Engine is a local app for chatting and roleplaying with AI characters. It runs on your own machine, and you connect it to an AI service of your choice. You create or import characters, pick how you want to chat, and talk to them. Marinara does not run an AI by itself, so your first job is to add a connection. Most people connect to an online AI provider. There is also a small built-in **Local Model** you can download. It is best for light helper tasks, not for your main chat; see [Local Model Setup](../connections/local-model.md). After that first setup, everything works from the app.

## The Home screen at a glance

The Home screen is what you see when no chat is open. It looks a bit like a web browser: tabs and bookmarks at the top, and a desk of widgets below.

At the top you find these parts:

- **Tabs.** **Home** is the main page. **Professor** opens Professor Mari, the built-in assistant who can explain the app and help you set things up. Some installed agents add a tab of their own.
- **Search bar.** On a computer, click the bar under the tabs, or press Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac), to find chats, characters, settings and more. Phones, tablets and very narrow windows don't show this bar, in portrait or landscape.
- **Bookmarks.** Quick links to **Discord**, **Support**, **Credits**, **Documentation**, **Tutorial**, **FAQ**, **Achievements** and **Widgets**. **Achievements** shows only when the Achievements setting is on. Bookmarks appear only on the **Home** tab. On a phone, a tablet or a very narrow window, tap the bookmark button at the left of the tabs to see them as a list.

In the middle you see the app logo, the words **Marinara Engine** and the version number. Below them, the **Conversation**, **Roleplay** and **Game** buttons each start a new chat in that mode.

The first time you open Marinara, a short guided tour points out the main buttons and chat modes. For the full walkthrough, see [The First-Time Tutorial](tutorial.md).

## Home widgets

The rest of the Home screen is made of widgets: small cards you can show, hide and move around. These widgets come with the app:

| Widget | What it does | Shown at first |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Professor Mari** | Click **Ask Professor Mari** to open her chat. | Yes |
| **Recent chats** | Shows your latest chats with their last message. Click one to reopen it. If you have none yet, it says **Your chats will show here**. | Yes |
| **Learn the engine** | Links to **Documentation**, **Replay tutorial** and **Read FAQ**. | Yes |
| **What's New** | **See what's new** opens the What's New window for this version again, even if you closed it before. **Read release notes** opens the full release notes on the web. | Yes |
| **Community** | Links to **Discord**, **Support** and **Credits**. | Yes |
| **Character Library** | A fresh selection of your characters each day. | No |
| **Discovery desk** | Suggests agents you have not installed yet. Use the arrows to see more, or **Browse agents** to open the catalog. | No |
| **Character of the Day** | One character from your library, with **Start a chat** and **View character**. | No |
| **Clock & calendar** | Today's date and time. | No |
| **Achievements** | Your latest and closest achievements. Only available when the Achievements setting is on. | No |

Some installed agents add widgets too. Professor Mari can also make a personal widget for you if you ask her.

To choose your widgets, click **Widgets** in the bookmarks. The **Home Widgets** window lists them under **Built-in**, **Agents** and **Personal**. Click the eye button beside a widget to show or hide it. A widget you show again goes to the first free place on Home. Personal widgets also have buttons to edit or delete them.

To move a widget, drag the handle in its top-right corner. On a computer, the handle appears when you point at the widget. With a keyboard, focus the handle and use the arrow keys. Your widget layout is saved in this browser.

At the bottom of the **Home Widgets** window, **Browser chrome** has one switch: **Show URL bar** shows or hides the search bar on a computer.

## Your first steps

Follow these steps in order to get to your first chat.

1. Install Marinara Engine and open it in your browser. Pick your platform in [Marinara Engine Installation](../INSTALLATION.md).
2. Add a connection so the app can reach an AI service. A connection stores your provider, your API key, and your model. An API key is a secret code, a bit like a password, that you get from the AI provider. See [Connecting to an AI Provider](../connections/connecting-to-a-provider.md).
3. Make or import a character to chat with. Open the **Characters** panel, then click **New** to open the **Create Character** window, or click **Import** to bring in a card from a file. See [Creating and Editing Characters](../characters/creating-and-editing-characters.md).
4. Start a chat. In the chat sidebar, pick a mode tab, then click the **+** button. The button is named **New Conversation**, **New Roleplay**, or **New Game**, to match the tab. The new chat opens with a setup window that walks you through the rest. If you have no connection saved yet, a **Set Up Conversation**, **Set Up Roleplay**, or **Set Up Game** window appears first. Choose your connection there, then click **Create Chat**.

If you get stuck on any step, ask the built-in assistant. Read [Professor Mari, Your In-App Assistant](professor-mari.md) to see what she can do.

## The three chat modes

Marinara has three chat modes. You pick one when you start a new chat.

- **Conversation**: a plain, direct AI chat with no roleplay, styled like a messaging app. See [Conversation Mode: Getting Started](../conversation/getting-started.md).
- **Roleplay**: immersive roleplay with characters, scene tracking, and world state. See [Roleplay Mode: Getting Started](../roleplay/getting-started.md).
- **Game**: a single-player role-playing game run by an AI Game Master, with a party, dice, maps, and quests. See [Game Mode: Getting Started](../game/getting-started.md).

## Where to get help

You have several ways to get help without leaving the app:

- Ask **Professor Mari** on the Home screen for setup help and how-to answers.
- Read the **FAQ** on the Home screen for quick answers to common questions.
- Open the in-app guides with the **Documentation** bookmark on the Home screen.
- Click the **Discord** bookmark to reach the community, or **Support** to back the project.

If something is broken or does not work, see [Troubleshooting Marinara Engine](../TROUBLESHOOTING.md).
