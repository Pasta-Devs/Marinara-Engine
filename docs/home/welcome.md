# Getting Started with Marinara Engine

Marinara Engine is an app for chatting and roleplaying with AI characters. It runs on your own computer. This guide shows the Home screen and the steps to your first chat.

## Start your first chat

Before you start: you need an API key (a secret code, like a password) from an AI service, or the **Built-in model** described below.

1. Install Marinara Engine and open it in your browser. Pick your platform in [Marinara Engine Installation](../INSTALLATION.md).
2. Add an account. An account is one API key for one AI service. See [Connecting to an AI Provider](../connections/connecting-to-a-provider.md).
3. Pick the models the account should use. The same guide shows how.
4. Make or import a character. Open **Library → Characters** (click **Library** at the top right), then click **New character**, or click **Import** to bring in a card from a file. See [Creating and Editing Characters](../characters/creating-and-editing-characters.md).
5. Click **Conversation**, **Roleplay** or **Game** on Home. Or open your chats: click **Library** at the top right, pick **Chats** at the top of the sidebar, choose the **Convo**, **Roleplay** or **Game** tab and click **New**.
6. Follow the setup window that opens, then click **Create Chat**.

What you see: the new chat opens, and you can write your first message.

If you have no account yet, the add-account window opens first and ends with **Start chatting**. If a **Set Up** window asks you to choose an account, pick one from the list, then click **Create Chat**.

Marinara does not include an online AI. Most people connect to an online service. You can also download the **Built-in model**, a small model that runs on your computer with no API key. It suits light helper jobs, not your main chat. See [Built-in model setup](../connections/local-model.md).

The first time you open Marinara, a short guided tour starts. See [The First-Time Tutorial](tutorial.md). Stuck on a step? Ask Professor Mari, the built-in assistant: [Professor Mari, Your In-App Assistant](professor-mari.md).

## Finding your way around

Marinara has two fixed places, and they stay put whatever theme you use:

- **The top bar.** On the left, **Home** and a tab for each chat, character or page you open. On the right, the search button (or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd>, <kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> on a Mac) finds or opens anything: a chat, a character, a setting, a guide, or a question for Professor Mari. Next to it are **Chat settings** while a chat is open, **Docs** for these guides, and **Library**, which shows or hides the sidebar.
- **The sidebar** on the right, with a **Chats | Library** switch at its top. **Chats** lists your chats under the **Convo**, **Roleplay** and **Game** tabs (see [Managing Your Chat List](../chats/managing-chats.md)). **Library** lists **Characters**, **Personas**, **Lorebooks**, **Presets**, **Regex scripts**, **Connections**, **Agents** and **Custom tools**, then **Settings** and **Extensions**. Pick one to open its page in the middle.

On a computer you can also show two pages side by side. See [Split view](tabs-and-drafts.md#split-view-two-pages-at-once).

On a phone or tablet, **Home** is at the top left and **Library** (the stack button) at the top right. Library slides the sidebar over the page, with the same **Chats | Library** switch; its Library view has **Settings**, **Docs** and **All Tabs** at the bottom. Tap the title in the top bar to search, and the number button to see your open tabs. See [Tabs, Split View and Unsaved Changes](tabs-and-drafts.md) and [Touch gestures](../chats/messages.md#touch-gestures).

## The Home screen at a glance

Home shows when no chat is open. At the top are a greeting, the version number and **Conversation**, **Roleplay** and **Game**, which each start a new chat in that mode. With no model set up yet, **Connect a model to start chatting** opens the Connections page. **Professor** opens Professor Mari in her own tab, an installed app (such as Story Writer) adds a button here too, and **Widgets** arranges the cards below.

In the classic layout (switched off under **Settings → Appearance → Theme → New layout**), Home looks like a web browser instead, with tabs, a search bar and bookmarks for **Discord**, **Support**, **Credits**, **Documentation**, **Tutorial**, **FAQ**, **Achievements** and **Widgets**.

## Choose a chat mode

You pick a mode when you start a chat.

- **Conversation**: a plain AI chat, styled like a messaging app. See [Conversation Mode: Getting Started](../conversation/getting-started.md).
- **Roleplay**: a story with one or more characters. The app keeps track of the scene, such as place and time. See [Roleplay Mode: Getting Started](../roleplay/getting-started.md).
- **Game**: a single-player game. The AI acts as the Game Master who tells the story. You get a group of companions (a party), dice rolls, maps and quests. See [Game Mode: Getting Started](../game/getting-started.md).

## Home widgets

Widgets are small cards you can show, hide and move.

1. Click **Widgets** on Home. The **Home Widgets** window opens. It lists widgets under **Built-in**, **Agents** and **Personal**.
2. Click the eye button beside a widget to show or hide it. A widget you show again goes to the first free place on Home.
3. To move a widget, drag the handle in its top-right corner. On a computer, the handle appears when you point at the widget.

Your layout is saved in this browser. Personal widgets also have buttons to edit or delete them. In the classic layout, **Browser chrome** at the bottom of the window has one switch: **Show URL bar** shows or hides Home's search bar on a computer.

Professor Mari can make a personal widget for you if you ask her. Installed agents can add widgets too.

## Things to try

The **Things to try** widget suggests the next three features to try. Each task builds on the one before it: a gallery picture becomes a sticker, and the sticker gets sent in a chat.

- Click a task to open the spot in the app. If it has no spot, its guide opens.
- Click the check to mark it done, or the cross to skip it. A skipped task never shows again.
- Most tasks tick themselves when Marinara sees you've done them, such as a chat in that mode or a sticker label. Tick the others yourself.
- **See all** lists every task by trail, with **Show me**. A trail's tasks show once the tasks they build on are done. Trails under **Only in this layout** cover features of the new layout.
- Finishing a trail unlocks its **Explorer** achievement.

The widget follows **Feature tips** in **Settings > General > Confirmations & Help**. With tips off, it shows the guide links. Progress is saved in this browser.

### AI basics and Deep dives

**See all** has two more tabs:

- **AI basics**: a short tour for people new to AI. It explains what a model is, the kinds Marinara uses, tokens and context, and helps you set up a model if you want to (you can skip that step). It ends with Professor Mari. It's offered once, in a small window after the welcome tutorial. See [AI Basics](ai-basics.md).
- **Deep dives**: settings where the choice isn't obvious, and features few people find, such as CSS in a card's Creator's Notes. Groups you use most are listed first.

Each one opens its guide at the passage that matters, highlighted, with a card under it that explains it in plain words. Click **Got it** to tick it; in AI basics this opens the next stop. Once you finish the Core trail, the widget's last row suggests a deep dive. Finishing AI basics or a group of deep dives unlocks an **Explorer** achievement. The offer window also follows **Feature tips**.

## Where to get help

- Ask Professor Mari on the Home screen.
- Open **See all** in the **Things to try** widget, then **Read FAQ**, for quick answers.
- Click **Docs** in the top bar for the in-app guides.
- Click **Discord** in the **Community** widget to reach the community, or **Support** to back the project.

If something is broken, see [Troubleshooting Marinara Engine](../TROUBLESHOOTING.md).
