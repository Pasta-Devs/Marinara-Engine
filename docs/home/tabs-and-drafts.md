# Tabs, Split View and Unsaved Changes

In the new layout, every chat, character, persona, lorebook, preset and app you open gets a tab, so you can hop between them. This guide covers moving tabs around, tab groups, seeing two pages side by side, and what happens to changes you have not saved yet.

## Find your tabs

- **Computer and tablet held sideways:** the tabs sit in the top bar. Click one to go back to it.
- **Phone and tablet held upright:** tap the number button at the top right. **All Tabs** shows every tab as a card. Tap a card to open it, or tap **Done** to go back.
- **Swipe the top bar** left or right on a phone to go to the next or previous tab.

## Move, group and close tabs

Right-click a tab on a computer, or press and hold it on a touch screen, to open its menu:

- **Move left** and **Move right** move the tab one place. On a computer you can also drag it.
- **Add to new group** puts the tab in a group. On a computer you can also drag a tab onto another tab and rest it there for a moment: when the other tab lights up, drop it and the two become a group (drop it on a group to add it). A quick drop only moves the tab. Holding Shift while you drop groups straight away. While you are on a grouped tab, the group's tabs show in a second row under the top bar. Click the group's name to rename or close it.
- **Close tab** closes it, and **Close other tabs** closes the rest.
- **Reopen closed tab** brings back the tab you closed last.

On a phone, close a tab with the **X** on its card in **All Tabs**.

## Split view: two pages at once

On a computer, you can show two pages side by side, such as a chat and the character you are editing.

1. Drag a tab to the far left or right edge of the page. You can also drag Home, a Library section (Settings too), a chat from the Chats list, or the **Docs** button.
2. Drop it. The page splits: what you dragged on that side, and the page you were on beside it.

Or right-click a tab (press and hold on a touch screen) and choose **Open in split view**.

Each side works on its own: a reply can stream on one side while you edit on the other. Saves on either side show on the other at once.

- **The second side's bar** shows what it holds, a **Chat settings** button for a chat, then **Swap sides**, **Show only this page** and **Close split view**.
- **Tabs:** the top bar's tabs, the Chats list and the Library open things on the main side. The tab shown on the second side is marked; click it to trade the two sides. Closing either tab ends the split.
- **Size:** drag the divider between the sides. Double-click it to make them even. With the divider focused, the arrow keys move it too.
- **Narrow second side:** below about 900 pixels it switches to the tablet layout, and below about 560 to the phone layout.
- **Settings** work on either side. A setting changed on one side shows on the other at once, and each side keeps its own open category.

Split view needs room for two pages. Phones never split. A tablet held sideways splits once its side panels are closed. When the window gets too narrow, the second page is put away, and the split comes back when there is room again.

## Unsaved changes

When you edit a character, persona, lorebook, preset, agent, connection or regex script and leave it without saving (another tab, a chat, Home or a Library section), your changes wait in that tab. Nothing asks you first:

- The tab shows a small **dot** next to its name, in the top bar and in **All Tabs**, even after a reload.
- Go back to the tab and your changes are there again, still unsaved. Press **Save** to keep them.
- Your changes are also kept in this browser about a second after you stop typing (at most five seconds apart while you type), so a reload, a crash or a closed tab loses at most a moment of typing. After a reload, the editor shows a bar with **Restore** and **Discard**.

If the same card was saved on another device after you started your changes, **Restore** puts back only the fields you changed and keeps the rest of the newer version.

### Closing a tab with unsaved changes

Closing the editor you are in asks **Save**, **Don't save** or **Keep editing**. Closing a tab whose changes are waiting asks:

- **Open to save** goes to the tab so you can press **Save**.
- **Don't save** throws the changes away and closes the tab.
- **Keep tab** leaves everything as it was.

**Close other tabs** and **Close group** never close a tab with waiting changes. They tell you how many they kept.

Deleting a character, persona, lorebook, preset, agent or regex script closes its tab and drops any changes waiting for it.

### Editing a message

A message you are editing is kept too. If you go to another chat or tab before you press **Save** or **Cancel**, the editor is open again with your text when you come back.
