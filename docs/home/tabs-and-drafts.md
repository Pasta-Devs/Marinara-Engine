# Tabs and Unsaved Changes

In the new layout, every chat, character, persona, lorebook, preset and app you open gets a tab, so you can hop between them. This guide covers moving tabs around, tab groups, and what happens to changes you have not saved yet.

## Find your tabs

- **Computer and tablet held sideways:** the tabs sit in the top bar. Click one to go back to it.
- **Phone and tablet held upright:** tap the number button at the top right. **All Tabs** shows every tab as a card. Tap a card to open it, or tap **Done** to go back.
- **Swipe the top bar** left or right on a phone to go to the next or previous tab.

## Move, group and close tabs

Right-click a tab on a computer, or press and hold it on a touch screen, to open its menu:

- **Move left** and **Move right** move the tab one place. On a computer you can also drag it.
- **Add to new group** puts the tab in a group. While you are on a grouped tab, the group's tabs show in a second row under the top bar. Click the group's name to rename or close it.
- **Close tab** closes it, and **Close other tabs** closes the rest.
- **Reopen closed tab** brings back the tab you closed last.

On a phone, close a tab with the **X** on its card in **All Tabs**.

## Unsaved changes

When you edit a character, persona, lorebook, preset, agent, connection or regex script and go to another tab without saving, your changes wait in that tab:

- The tab shows a small **ring** next to its name.
- Go back to the tab and your changes are there again, still unsaved. Press **Save** to keep them.
- Your changes are also kept in this browser while you type, so a reload or a crash loses at most a second or so. After a reload, the editor shows a bar with **Restore** and **Discard**.

If the same card was saved on another device after you started your changes, **Restore** puts back only the fields you changed and keeps the rest of the newer version.

### Closing a tab with unsaved changes

Closing a tab whose changes are waiting asks first:

- **Open to save** goes to the tab so you can press **Save**.
- **Don't save** throws the changes away and closes the tab.
- **Keep tab** leaves everything as it was.

**Close other tabs** never closes a tab with waiting changes. It tells you how many it kept.

Deleting a character, persona, lorebook, preset, agent or regex script closes its tab and drops any changes waiting for it.

### Editing a message

A message you are editing is kept too. If you go to another chat or tab before you press **Save** or **Cancel**, the editor is open again with your text when you come back.
