# The First-Time Tutorial

The tutorial is a guided tour that starts the first time you open Marinara Engine. Professor Mari, the built-in assistant, walks you around the main parts of the app. Use this guide to skip the tour, replay it, or see what each step covers.

## Replay the tutorial

1. Open **Settings → General → Confirmations & Help**.
2. Under **Walkthroughs**, click **Replay the welcome tour**.

You can also click **Replay tutorial** in the **Learn the engine** widget on Home. The tour starts again from the first step.

**Walkthroughs** has two more buttons. **Replay the computer walkthrough** (or tablet, or phone) replays the short tour for the kind of device you are on, and **Show chat mode tips again** brings back each chat mode's tips. See [Walkthroughs for each device](#walkthroughs-for-each-device).

## Skip the tutorial

Every card has a skip button. On the first card it reads **Skip Tutorial**. On later cards it reads **Skip**. Click it to end the tour at once.

- **Skip**, **Skip Tutorial** and the last card's button all mark the tour as done, so it does not start again by itself. They can also unlock the **Diligent Student** achievement.
- The last card has a list for the language of the in-app guides. Only the last card's button saves it; **Skip** leaves it as it was. You can change it later in **Settings → General → Language → Documentation Language**.

## How the tour works

The tour shows one card at a time. Each card has a title, a short explanation and progress dots. On a computer, a pulsing ring highlights the part of the screen the card talks about, and some steps open that part for you. For example, the **Connections** step opens the Connections page.

Click **Next** to go to the next card.

On a phone or other narrow screen, each card shows centered below the top bar, with no pulsing ring.

## Reference: the steps

| # | Card title | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Welcome to Marinara Engine!** | Professor Mari offers to show you around. |
| 2 | **Your Chats** | The chats sidebar and the speech-bubble button that opens it, and **Chat settings** inside a chat. |
| 3 | **Three Ways to Chat** | The **Convo**, **Roleplay** and **Game** tabs and what each mode is for. |
| 4 | **The Library** | The Library on the right: Characters, Personas, Lorebooks, Presets and Regex scripts. |
| 5 | **Connections: Set This Up First** | Opens the Connections page. Add a connection before you chat. |
| 6 | **Agents** | Opens the Agents page, where you install optional features. |
| 7 | **Settings (and SillyTavern Imports)** | Opens **Settings** on the **Data** tab, where SillyTavern imports are. |
| 8 | **Tabs and Search** | The tabs in the top bar and **+** / <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd>. |
| 9 | **The Docs, and You're Set!** | The **Docs** button, and the language list for the guides. |

On a phone or tablet the tour adds a **Handy Gestures** card (ten steps in all), and the cards say where things are on a touch screen: the Library behind the stack button, search behind the title.

## Walkthroughs for each device

Each kind of device (computer, tablet, phone) gets a short walkthrough the first time Marinara opens on it, even if you finished the full tour somewhere else. It shows where the chats, the Library and search are on that device, and on a touch screen the **Handy Gestures** card. Each chat mode also shows its own tips the first time you open it on that device. Replay them from **Settings → General → Confirmations & Help → Walkthroughs**.
