# Keyboard Shortcuts and Touch Gestures

Marinara answers to keys on a computer and to swipes and pulls on a phone or tablet. This guide lists all of them. The same lists are in **Settings › Shortcuts**, and pressing <kbd>?</kbd> anywhere (while you are not typing in a box) opens that page.

On a Mac, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> in this guide means <kbd>⌘</kbd> and <kbd>Alt</kbd> means <kbd>⌥</kbd> (Option), unless a line says otherwise.

## Going back and forward

Marinara remembers the pages you visit, like a web browser does: chats, characters, lorebooks, presets, Library sections, Docs, Professor Mari and Home. You can step back through them and forward again.

- **Computer:** click the **←** and **→** arrows next to the logo at the top left, press <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>←</kbd> and <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>→</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>[</kbd> and <kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>]</kbd> on a Mac), or use the back and forward buttons on the side of your mouse.
- **Phone and tablet:** swipe in from the left edge of the screen, or use your browser's or Android's back button. Each step closes what is on top: a menu, a sheet, Chat settings, an editor, a Library page, then back to Home.

A few things to know:

- Visiting a new page after going back drops the pages that were ahead, as in a browser.
- A page whose tab you closed is skipped.
- When there is nothing to go back to, the arrow is greyed out, and the key and mouse button do what your browser normally does.
- Unsaved changes in an editor wait in its tab, just like when you switch tabs (see [Tabs, Split View and Unsaved Changes](tabs-and-drafts.md)).

## Keyboard shortcuts

### Moving around

These work on a computer, or on a tablet held sideways with a keyboard attached.

| Shortcut                                                                            | What it does                          |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>←</kbd>, or the mouse's back button                             | Back to the page you were on before   |
| <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>→</kbd>, or the mouse's forward button                          | Forward again after going back        |
| <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>←</kbd> or <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Page Up</kbd>   | Previous tab                          |
| <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>→</kbd> or <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Page Down</kbd> | Next tab                              |
| <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>8</kbd>                        | Go to that tab, counted from the left |
| <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>9</kbd>                                        | Go to the last tab                    |
| <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>W</kbd>                                                         | Close the tab on screen               |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>T</kbd>                                       | Reopen the tab you closed last        |

The tab keys use <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd> because browsers keep <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Tab</kbd>, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>W</kbd> and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>9</kbd> for their own tabs, and Chrome and Firefox on Linux also keep <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>9</kbd>. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>T</kbd> only reaches Marinara in Firefox or when Marinara is installed as an app; elsewhere use **Reopen closed tab** in a tab's right-click menu.

### Everywhere

| Shortcut                                   | What it does                                                                   |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd>               | Open or close Search                                                           |

| <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>M</kbd>                | Open the model chooser in the message box                                      |

| <kbd>Esc</kbd>                             | Close the open menu, popover or dialog                                         |

## Touch gestures

| Gesture                                   | What it does                                                    |
| ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Swipe in from the left edge               | Go back one step                                                |
| Swipe the top bar's title left or right   | Next or previous tab                                            |
| Pull the top bar down                     | Open Search, or Professor Mari if you pull toward her           |
| Pull the Chats list down from its top     | Reload the list, with chats made on your other devices          |
| Swipe the newest reply left or right      | Change replies (past the last one asks before making a new one) |
| Swipe a chat row left                     | Pin, Rename, Tags and Delete                                    |
| Press and hold a message, chat row or tab | Open its menu                                                   |

### The edge swipe in the Home Screen app

In Safari, the left-edge swipe is Safari's own, and Marinara makes each swipe step back one level instead of leaving the app. An app added to the Home Screen with **Share › Add to Home Screen** has no back swipe of its own, so there Marinara draws its own: a round arrow follows your finger and turns colored once you have pulled far enough. Let go to go back, or slide back to the edge to cancel. In right-to-left languages it comes from the right edge.

### Every gesture has a button

You never need a gesture:

- **Back:** the **‹** button at the top of a page, or **Home**.
- **Changing tabs:** the tabs button at the top right shows **All Tabs**.
- **Search:** the magnifier in the top bar.
- **Refreshing chats:** **Refresh the list** in the Chats list's **⋯** menu (on a computer, the circular arrow at the top of the Chats list).
- **Replies:** the **Reply 1 of 3** chip on the newest reply shows its other versions.
- **Chat row actions:** press and hold the row.

### Turning swipes off

**Settings › Shortcuts › Touch Gestures** has a switch, **Swipe gestures on touch screens**. Turning it off stops the title, reply and chat row swipes, so a sideways drag only scrolls. Back from the edge, pulling down, and pressing and holding keep working.
