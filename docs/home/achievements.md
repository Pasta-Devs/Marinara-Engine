# Achievements

Achievements are cosmetic badges that unlock as you use Marinara Engine, for example when you create chats or visit community links. They do not change how the app works. This guide shows where to find them, the full list, and how to turn them off.

## Open the Achievements window

1. Go to the Home screen.
2. Click the **Achievements** bookmark (trophy icon). On a phone, tap the bookmark button at the left of the tabs first.

The **Achievements** window opens. At the top is a summary such as **12 of 24 achievements unlocked in this profile.** A profile is your saved set of Marinara data. Below it is a grid of cards.

- A locked card shows **?????** and a lock icon. You cannot read it until you unlock it.
- An unlocked card shows an **unlocked** tag.
- A card with a target also shows a **Progress** row and bar.

You can also add the **Achievements** widget to Home. Click **Widgets** in the bookmarks and turn it on. It shows a count like **12 of 24 unlocked**, your **Last obtained** achievement and your **Closest next** one. While it loads, it reads **Checking the collection...**. Click it to open the window. See [Home widgets](welcome.md#home-widgets).

If it did not work: the bookmark, widget and window show only when the **Achievements** setting is on. See [Turn achievements on or off](#turn-achievements-on-or-off).

Your progress is saved in the profile you use. A different profile or a fresh install starts with nothing unlocked. Unlocks are saved in a full backup.

## The 24 achievements

There are 24, in four categories: **Milestone**, **Community**, **Creation** and **Collection**. **Creation** and **Collection** achievements have three tiers, **I**, **II** and **III**, at 5, 25 and 100. A badge title can read **Hoarder II** once you reach the second tier.

| Achievement | Category | How you unlock it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Diligent Student** | Milestone | Finish or skip the first-time tutorial. |
| **Hello World** | Milestone | Send your first message to Professor Mari. |
| **Please Handle With Care** | Milestone | Drag Professor Mari's picture around the screen. |
| **One Of Us** | Community | Click the **Discord** bookmark, or the **Discord** link in the **Community** widget. |
| **Based Backer** | Community | Click **Support** (it opens Ko-fi) in the bookmarks or the **Community** widget. |
| **Backseat Appreciator** | Community | Click **Credits** in the bookmarks or the **Community** widget. |
| **Who Needs IRL Friends** | Creation | Create Conversation chats (5, 25, 100). |
| **They Feel Real To Me** | Creation | Create Roleplay chats (5, 25, 100). |
| **I Have No Other Hobbies** | Creation | Create Game chats (5, 25, 100). |
| **Hoarder** | Collection | Collect characters (5, 25, 100). |
| **The World's A Stage** | Collection | Collect lorebooks (5, 25, 100). |
| **I Am A Gamer** | Collection | Collect personas (5, 25, 100). |

- A persona is the character card that stands for you in a chat. A lorebook is a set of world facts the AI reads when their keywords come up.
- **Collection** counts everything of that type in your library, including imported and downloaded items. For **Hoarder**, the built-in Professor Mari character never counts.
- For the community achievements, visiting the link is enough. You do not need to sign up on the other site.

## Achievements from agents

Installed agents can add their own achievements. They show in the same window, after the built-in ones, under a heading such as **From Noodle**. They follow the same rules, and an agent can show its own badge art.

If you remove an agent, its section goes away and your unlocks are kept. If you install it again, they show as unlocked again. The count at the top includes the agents you have installed.

## Turn achievements on or off

1. Open **Settings → General**.
2. In the **App Behavior** section, turn the **Achievements** toggle on or off. It is on by default.

- On: the bookmark and window are available, and a pop-up appears when something unlocks.
- Off: the bookmark, widget and pop-ups are hidden. The app still records unlocks in the background, so nothing is lost when you turn it back on.

## How unlocking works

Marinara checks your counts when a tracked action happens, such as creating a chat or clicking a bookmark, and when you open the window.

- When an action unlocks a badge and the setting is on, a pop-up titled **Achievement unlocked** names the badge, for example **Hoarder II**.
- Opening the window can unlock badges you already earned another way. These are silent: the card shows as unlocked with no pop-up.
- An unlocked badge stays unlocked, even if you later delete chats, characters, lorebooks or personas.
- The **Progress** bar on an unlocked card shows your current count, so a card can read **2 / 5** while the badge is earned. That is normal.

There is no button to reset achievements.
