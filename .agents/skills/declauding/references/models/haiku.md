## Editing a draft Haiku wrote

The linter reaches more of a Haiku draft than of any other model's. Start there.

1. Work through the full lint report: participle tails (25), bolded
   inline-header lists (29), Title Case headers (30), negation-first (2).
2. Rejoin one-line drama paragraphs to their neighbours (entry 9): *We tracked
   the wrong things.* standing alone.
3. Delete the announcement before a payload and start with the payload (entries
   8 and 38): *Here's the thing nobody tells you:*
4. Read the closers anyway; Haiku ends sections on maxims too: *Measure first,
   always.*
